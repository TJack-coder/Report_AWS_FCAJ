## Overall evaluation

### Working environment

AWS Viet Nam provided a professional, innovative, and welcoming environment. The workspace and learning resources supported sustained focus and development throughout the program.

### Mentor and administrator support

The mentor encouraged independent problem-solving while remaining available when blockers appeared, especially around Docker, ECR, and CI/CD. The administrator team ensured smooth onboarding and timely access to resources.

### Relevance to my major

CloudLibrary aligned with Computer Science and Software Engineering coursework while extending my knowledge of practical cloud infrastructure and DevOps.

### Development opportunities

- Improved React, Vite, Docker, and AWS skills.
- Practiced Agile workflows and team collaboration.
- Developed corporate communication and structured problem-solving.

## Additional questions

### Most satisfying achievement

The most rewarding moment was seeing CloudLibrary running on Elastic Beanstalk and the automated CI/CD pipeline complete successfully after the technical issues had been resolved.

### What should the company improve?

The FCAJ program was already well structured. The main recommendation is to continue providing end-to-end practical projects and strong support for future cohorts.

### Would I recommend the program?

Yes. It provides exposure to cloud technologies, direct learning from industry professionals, and opportunities to contribute to a meaningful project.

## Suggestions and expectations

- Continue the end-to-end project-based learning model.
- Provide opportunities for advanced programs or follow-up projects.
- Maintain technical sharing sessions and hands-on mentoring.
