This article analyzes the use of `useMemo` for large lists, combining searches across title, author, category, ISBN, and shelf location. Multi-condition filtering and `localeCompare`-based sorting support Vietnamese text and improve the lookup experience.

**Keywords:** React, useMemo, multi-field search, filtering, localeCompare.
